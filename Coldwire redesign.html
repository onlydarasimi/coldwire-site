The problem

The founders are the sales team.

Most software companies in this space have a handful of salespeople, sometimes none. The people who could be prospecting are busy running demos, onboarding and support.

02Word of mouth

Referrals have a ceiling.

Reputation and introductions fill the first stretch. Then they slow down, and nothing is built underneath to keep new conversations coming.

03Setup is the work

Outbound is a machine nobody has time to build.
